Album structure · correcting the silhouette

Which object actually reads as an album?

The current scene fails because its left “binding” is empty space inside a rounded card. These alternatives establish the physical object before we compress the eight screens.

A · RECOMMENDED
Our first page

True two-page spread

A visible center fold and two curved page planes. The left page carries quiet context; the active keepsake lives on the right. Clearly an album without a fake gutter.

B · MOST EDITORIAL
The beginning of your family story.
Layered handmade sheets, with no outer container at all.

Loose scrapbook page

Removes the book metaphor and lets paper layers float directly in the screen. Beautiful and compact, but reads as a keepsake/scrapbook more than an album.

C · MOST COMPACT
A chapter begins here.

Open folio crop

One active page with the cover peeking underneath and chapter tabs on the edge. Strong silhouette and more room for controls, but less emotionally rich than a spread.

What changes regardless: remove the internal left gutter, stop rounding the whole thing like an app card, give pages distinct edges and a real seam/cover relationship, and let the object occupy the canvas instead of sitting inside another padded container.
